How Ordering Works

  1. i.

    Tell us about it

    Start an order online and pick your unit from the list. Ordering for someone else, or keeping it a surprise? Just say so on the form and we'll only contact you.

    Get us your items

    Drop them off at 50 Public Square, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm, or mail them to us if you're out of town or already moved.

    We build it

    We'll confirm the details and give you an estimated completion date with your quote. Pick it up at the shop, or we can ship it.

Before you order

Good to Know

Order early

We prefer at least 2 weeks. If you have a ceremony date, add it to your order so we can plan around it. In a rush? Call us at 315-773-3758.

Missing medals or insignia?

Tell us what's missing in your order and we'll let you know your options. Company-specific coins and patches come from your unit, so you'll need to supply those.

Moving or deployed?

Order online and mail your items. We can ship the finished piece to you. See our packing tips on the Help page.
